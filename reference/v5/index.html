09 · FAQs

Questions we hear most

Something else? Call +91 98807 03083, write to info@saelenergy.com, or read the SAEL blog.

Is my home eligible for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Residential on-grid and hybrid rooftop systems qualify. The central subsidy is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW, up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. We check eligibility for free and file the application for you.

On-grid, off-grid or hybrid: which do I need?

On-grid suits homes, shops and offices with a reliable grid: it's the lowest-cost option. Hybrid adds a battery for homes with frequent power cuts. Off-grid suits places with no grid connection at all. The finder above gives a quick suggestion.

What happens during a power cut?

An on-grid system switches off when the grid fails, for safety. A hybrid or off-grid system switches to its battery and keeps your essential loads running.

Can a grid-tie system run on its own?

No. A grid-tie (on-grid) system needs the grid to run, and it shuts down during a power cut so it can't send power into lines that crews are repairing. If you need backup, choose a hybrid system with a battery.

Will solar power leave my building?

Only the surplus. Your own loads use solar power first; whatever is left flows to the grid through the net meter, and your DISCOM credits it against your bill.

How is a solar pump sized?

By the head (depth) and the discharge you need. We supply 1 to 10 HP AC or DC pumps for heads up to 100 m, with a VFD controller that adapts to changing sunlight.

Can I go solar without paying upfront?

Yes. Under OpEx, investors fund the system and you pay for the power it produces. Under CapEx, you own the system, and we handle installation and liaisoning end to end.
